Assessment pipeline

Eight stages, every result traceable

Each stage records what it did — including what it skipped — so a decision can be reproduced and audited rather than trusted on faith.

01

DISCOVER

Static, read-only attack-surface discovery enumerates endpoints, integrations, environment references and risk-tagged surfaces from the target's source.

02

SCAN

Configured scanners and active probes run against the local target. Uninstalled scanners are skipped and recorded as coverage limitations — never silently.

03

NORMALIZE

Heterogeneous tool output is normalized into one finding model, each with a stable content fingerprint used as its identity.

04

CORRELATE

Findings describing the same weakness across tools are grouped so corroborating evidence is visible and duplicates collapse.

05

VERIFY

Active probes attempt to confirm or refute suspected weaknesses. Results are recorded as confirmed, refuted, inconclusive or not applicable.

06

SCORE

Each finding receives a WATCHTOWER Risk Score (watchtower-risk-score-v1) from severity, verification and asset factors. This is not CVSS.

07

EXPLAIN

Every finding carries a plain-language explanation, an evidence chain and an evidence-confidence rating (high / medium / low).

08

REPORT

A reproducible report.json / report.html / report.sarif is produced and a security gate renders a PASS / WARN / FAIL decision.

Capabilities

What WATCHTOWER actually does

Only implemented checks are described. Several external scanners are integrated but were not installed for the current assessment — they are shown as coverage limitations, not completed work.

Attack Surface Discovery

Static, read-only enumeration of endpoints, integrations and environment references. Risk tags mark candidate security-relevant surfaces — they are not vulnerability findings.

Evidence-Based Verification

Active probes exercise suspected weaknesses and record confirmed / refuted / inconclusive / not-applicable results. Inconclusive is never promoted to verified.

Multi-Scanner Correlation

Normalized findings are fingerprinted, de-duplicated and correlated across tools so shared evidence is grouped rather than double-counted.

Security Gate

A structured watchtower-gate-v1 decision (PASS / WARN / FAIL) driven by verified findings and coverage limitations, with an optional baseline for regression comparison.

Architecture

Target in, reproducible decision out

A single directional flow from the target application to a structured security-gate decision. Artifacts are emitted at the end; the gate is WATCHTOWER's own policy — not CVSS.

Evidence & lifecycle

From scanner signal to a defensible decision

Every finding moves through an explicit lifecycle. WATCHTOWER does not claim zero false positives or total verification — it states exactly what was confirmed, what was refuted, and what still needs a human.

Scanner finding Correlation Verification Evidence Lifecycle decision
Suspected

Reported by a scanner, not yet verified.

Correlated

Corroborated across more than one tool.

Verified

Confirmed by an active probe.

Needs manual review

Inconclusive — a human decision is required.

False positive

Refuted by evidence and recorded as such — not hidden.
